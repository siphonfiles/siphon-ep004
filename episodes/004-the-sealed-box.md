# Siphon Files — Episode 004: The Sealed Box

## ACT 2 — THE BOX  (~2:05–4:00)

[CARD: CHAPTER 1 · THE BOX]

**b1** A Ledger has two brains. [S15]

**b2** The first is a secure chip. It holds your keys, and it's built never to let them out. [S15]

**b3** The second is an ordinary chip. It runs the buttons, the Bluetooth. And the screen. [S15]

**b4** In twenty twenty, Ledger wrote down a rule for the Nano X. That second chip could be fully compromised, and the funds would still be safe. [S15]

**b5** Because the secret never leaves the secure chip. Except once.

**b6** When you set the device up, it shows you your recovery phrase. Twenty-four words. On the screen. So you can copy them down.

**THOMAS b7** For a few minutes, your most valuable secret is on display.

**b8** Now look behind that screen. [CARD: teardown, layered diagram]

**b9** Researchers who opened tampered units found an extra board, tucked where a strip of padding should be. A microcontroller. A cellular modem. An antenna. And an eSIM. [S5][S2]

**b10** To make room, the original battery had been swapped for a smaller one. [S7]

**b11** Ledger's own security team, the Donjon, described what the implant does. [S2]

**b12** It reads the data going to the screen. When it sees the recovery words, it decodes them. Then it sends them out over LTE. The mobile network. [S2]

**b13** It doesn't touch the secure chip. It doesn't change a single transaction. [S2]

**b14** And it doesn't need your laptop or your phone. It brought its own connection. [S2]

**THOMAS b15** No virus to catch. No link to click. Just a box.

**b16** Ledger's genuineness check is built to test the secure chip. And in these devices, the secure chip was the real one. [S2][S5]
